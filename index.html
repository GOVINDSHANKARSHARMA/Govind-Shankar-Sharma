01 — ABOUT ME

Curiosity, made practical.

I’m at the start of my engineering journey, with a simple goal: understand how things work, then learn to make them work better.

I’m pursuing a Diploma in Mechanical Engineering (Production) at Government Polytechnic Naraini, Banda, Uttar Pradesh. I’m interested in manufacturing processes, technical drawing, CAD, quality, and the practical decisions that turn a design into a useful product.

Alongside college, I explore AI tools, Python and web basics, and I enjoy researching, writing and structuring educational content. Current concepts include StudyAI, an AI notes generator for students, and a premium gaming website idea. I want to grow through hands-on training, real projects and feedback from experienced people.
